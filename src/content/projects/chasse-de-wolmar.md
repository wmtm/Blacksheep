## The brief

Domaine de Wolmar needed something a printed trail map can't do: a way to actually explore the terrain before setting foot on it, find and share a specific hunting placement, give newcomers a feel for the property before they arrive, and know what the wind is doing before a session starts. All of it from one link, no app install, and with the estate's own private data kept behind a passcode so the same page works for general orientation and for the estate's own private use.

## What it does

The whole ~585-hectare estate as a live satellite-terrain map with time-of-day lighting. Click "Isoler le domaine" and it drops into a dedicated 3D terrain cutaway, a separate scene built from real elevation data (public Copernicus DEM), with stratified cutaway walls, adjustable relief exaggeration and sun angle, and a slow orbit camera, closer to an architectural model than a typical web map. A historical-imagery slider lets you scrub the same terrain year by year. Live weather and a short-range wind forecast, with hourly speed, gusts and direction, designed for this audience rather than a generic widget. Distance, area and elevation measurement built directly into the terrain. Registered hunting stands ("chutes") and miradors stay private behind a passcode until an authorized visitor unlocks them.

## Where it comes from

It grows out of the same instinct as the illustrated maps: draw the place so it actually feels like the place. This one just happens to run in a browser, turn in three dimensions, and know which way the wind is blowing.
