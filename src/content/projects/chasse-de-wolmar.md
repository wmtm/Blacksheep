## The brief

Domaine de Wolmar needed something a printed trail map can't do: a way to actually explore the terrain before setting foot on it, check a ridge from another angle, see exactly where one stand's line of sight ends and the next begins, and know what the wind is doing before a drive starts.

## What it does

The whole ~585-hectare estate as an interactive map, with every shooting stand ("chute") and point of interest hand-placed and tracked. A from-scratch 3D terrain cutaway, not a flat image: real elevation data and satellite texture sculpted into a relief block you can orbit and adjust, like a scale model of the property. A historical-imagery time slider to compare the estate's satellite photos year over year. Live weather and an hourly wind forecast panel, readable at a glance. Distance and area measurement for planning a route or a drive for real.

## Where it comes from

It grows out of the same instinct as the illustrated maps: draw the place so it actually feels like the place. This one just happens to run in a browser, turn in three dimensions, and know which way the wind is blowing.
