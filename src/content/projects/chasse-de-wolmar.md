## The brief

Chasse de Wolmar needed something a printed trail map can't do: a way to actually explore the terrain before setting foot on it, zoom into a ridge, check a trail from another angle, see exactly where one hunting zone ends and the next begins.

## What it does

A fully interactive 3D model of the grounds, built from real topography, with trails, zones, landmarks and safety markers layered on top. Hunters, hikers and anyone heading into the bush can rotate it, zoom it, and plan a route for real, instead of guessing from a flat map pinned to a cabin wall.

## Where it comes from

It grows out of the same instinct as the illustrated maps: draw the place so it actually feels like the place. This one just happens to run in a browser and turn in three dimensions instead of sitting flat on a page.
