## The hardest client

Designing for yourself is supposed to be the fun, easy one. It never is — no brief to react to, no constraints to push against, just a blank page and an unreasonable number of opinions.

## What comes out of it

This is where new type pairings, colour ideas and motion techniques get tested before they're trusted on client work — a low-stakes place to find out if an idea actually holds up outside your head.

## Ongoing

New pieces get added whenever an idea refuses to leave me alone.
