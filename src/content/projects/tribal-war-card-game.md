## The pitch, to myself

Nobody commissioned this one. It started as "what if each tribe had a completely different silhouette language" and turned into a full card game system before there was any reason to stop.

## Building a cast

Each faction got its own shape grammar, palette and pose logic — sharp angles and cold colour for one tribe, rounded forms and warm tones for another — so you could recognise a card's allegiance from across a table, face-down.

## Why it matters

Client work has rules. This didn't. It's the project that keeps the illustration muscle honest.
