## The pitch, to myself

Nobody commissioned this one. It started as "what if a card game actually had lore" and turned into a full tactical system, in French, with its own mythology, before there was any reason to stop.

## How it plays

Four card types do four different jobs: attack cards fight close or at range, defense cards hold the line without striking back, ghost cards come from a shared pile both players can summon by sacrifice, and magic runs on ritual series, chains of spells where each choice trades a cost for an effect. Every card carries its own hand-drawn skull, horns and icon set, so you can read a fighter's role before you read a single word.

## Why it matters

Client work has rules. This didn't. It's the project that keeps the illustration muscle honest, and the only one with its own rulebook.
